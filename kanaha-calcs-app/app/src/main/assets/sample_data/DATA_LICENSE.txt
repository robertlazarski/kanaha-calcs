Tickers: MSFT, AAPL, AMZN, JPM, JNJ
Column: Adj. Close (split- and dividend-adjusted closing price)
Period: March 2008 - March 2018 (10 years, ~2,500 trading days)
Dataset last updated: March 27, 2018 (discontinued April 11, 2018)

Corporate action adjustment:
  Adj. Close retroactively modifies all historical prices to account
  for stock splits and dividend payments, ensuring return continuity.
  Notable events in this window:
    - AAPL: 7-for-1 split, June 9, 2014
    - MSFT: Quarterly dividends (~$0.20-$0.42/share)
    - JPM:  Quarterly dividends
    - JNJ:  Quarterly dividends (Dividend King, 50+ years of increases)
    - AMZN: No splits or dividends in this period
